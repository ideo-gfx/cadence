# Cadence Brand Guidelines

## Approved identity
Cadence uses the approved **Rhythm** mark: three rounded, angled capsules with a short left bar, tall center bar, and medium right bar. Do not substitute equalizer bars, dot grids, loops, or alternate concepts.

## Wordmark
Use the word **Cadence** in a bold geometric sans-serif. In product contexts, use Space Grotesk Bold (700). For PNG exports, the bundled raster uses a close available sans-serif rendering; the SVG wordmark specifies Space Grotesk with fallbacks.

## Brand idea
**The rhythm of work.**

## Palette
- Magenta: #B92D63
- Pink: #C73870
- Rose: #D44784
- Charcoal: #17151B
- Surface: #F8F6F9
- White: #FFFFFF
- Border: #E5DDE5
- Muted: #6D6670

## Usage
- Light logo: charcoal wordmark and colored Rhythm symbol on light surfaces.
- Dark logo: white wordmark and colored Rhythm symbol on charcoal.
- Symbol-only: use for app icons, favicons, compact navigation and avatars.
- Monochrome: use only where color reproduction is restricted.
- Maintain clear space around the mark at least the height of the short bar.
- Do not distort, rotate, rearrange bars, add outlines, or replace the capsule geometry.
