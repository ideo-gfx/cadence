# Cadence — Brand Kit

## Brand idea
**Cadence** is the rhythm of a team's work. The product brings daily updates, timesheets, leave, approvals, and team visibility into one calm workspace.

**Tagline:** The rhythm of work.

## Logo concept: Rhythm
Three rounded vertical bars form an abstract rhythm mark. Their differing heights suggest the varied pace of work, progress, and the daily cadence of a team. Keep the mark simple and recognizable at small sizes.

## Logo variants included
- Horizontal wordmark: light and dark backgrounds
- Stacked logo: light and dark backgrounds
- Rhythm symbol: primary, charcoal, and white
- Single-colour wordmarks: black, white, and magenta
- App icons and PNG sizes from 16px through 512px
- SVG source files are scalable and editable

The palette is inspired by the MELD design-system color direction. Treat these as Cadence product brand tokens; verify actual UI contrast before use in text or interactive controls.

## Typography
**Space Grotesk** — preferred family, weights 400, 500, 600, 700. Use 600 for the wordmark reference. For app UI, follow MELD typography tokens and avoid using display typography for dense data.

## Clear space and sizing
- Maintain clear space around the mark approximately equal to the width of one rhythm bar.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, or change the bar spacing.
- For small sizes, use the standalone rhythm mark. Avoid the full wordmark below approximately 120px wide.
- Prefer SVG for web, app, and print; use PNG for raster contexts.

## Background use
- Light: primary/pink/rose bars with charcoal wordmark on white or off-white.
- Dark: pink/primary/light-pink bars with white wordmark on charcoal.
- Monochrome: use black, white, or primary magenta only.

## Voice and personality
Calm, clear, human, organised, and quietly energetic. The product should feel like a helpful workplace tool—not a surveillance or HR bureaucracy product.

Suggested UI language: “Share your update”, “Submit timesheet”, “Request leave”, “Needs your review”, “Your week”.
